# Sensor recalibration

A service release. Roger now runs on the new version of its speech engine, and long recordings lose fewer words.

## Fixes

- **Long recordings could miss words.** In a meeting or a dropped file, speech at the very start could go missing, and so could a few words where Roger joins the parts of a long recording. Both are found and kept now.
- **An interrupted model download started over.** If the first download of the speech model stopped (sleep, lost Wi-Fi), Roger threw away what it had and began again. Now it goes on where it stopped.
- **No € with a language set.** On a preset with a fixed language, the € sign never made it into the text: the speech model heard it, but the language check swapped it for something else. Now you get your €.

## Changes

- **Updated speech engine.** Roger uses FluidAudio 0.18.3. Speaker detection works as before: one speaker per word.

## Breaking changes

None.
