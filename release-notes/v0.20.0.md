# Where we're going, we need progress bars

Drop screenshots during a meeting to get a timeline of slides and speech. Real progress bars everywhere.

## New & Noteworthy

- **Slide checkpoints.** Someone is sharing slides in your meeting? Drag a screenshot onto the recording overlay. Roger files it into the session folder and writes a transcript segment covering the audio since the last drop, everything named by timestamp so it sorts in order. When you stop, the full transcript weaves the screenshots in as inline images - the folder becomes a chronological record of what was said and shown, ready to hand to an AI agent.
- **Honest progress bars.** Model downloads now show real percentages in onboarding, Settings and the menu bar. Meeting finalisation and file transcription report actual speaker-identification progress instead of vibes.
- **F18 is taken.** The meeting recording hotkey now refuses F18. That key is your dictation trigger and giving one key two jobs ends poorly.
- Under the hood: FluidAudio 0.15.5 and KeyboardShortcuts 3.0.1.

## Fixes

- Diarization progress can no longer overshoot 100% or rewind. Progress bars only move forward, like time.
