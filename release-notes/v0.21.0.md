# Hailing frequencies open

A better ear for Roger and no more silent dictations from a muted mic.

## New & Noteworthy

- **Fewer wrong words.** Roger now listens with Parakeet Ultra, a post-trained Parakeet v3. Same 25 languages, same speed, about a fifth fewer errors across languages. On first launch Roger downloads the new model (about 630 MB). Dictation waits until it's ready. The old model is deleted once Ultra is loaded, so your disk doesn't pay twice.
- **A muted mic no longer means a silent dictation.** A mic muted at system level (often left over from a meeting) gave Roger nothing but silence, and nothing told you why. Roger now lifts the mute while you dictate and puts it back when you're done. A mute you set on purpose in a call stays in place.
- **Smaller app.** Roger no longer ships a text processing library it never used.
- **Your words stay out of the logs.** The speech engine's debug output no longer contains what you said.

## Fixes

- **Some dictations came back empty.** For certain recordings of around 11 to 13 seconds the speech model heard nothing at all. Those now get a second try with a slightly different length.
- **A crash during a meeting no longer leaves your mic muted.** If Roger quit unexpectedly while the meeting mute was on, the mic stayed muted for every app. Roger now restores it on the next launch.
- **Unmuting in a meeting always unmutes.** If the mic was already muted when you muted it with the hotkey, unmuting kept it muted.

## Breaking changes

None. The one-time model download is the only thing you'll notice.
